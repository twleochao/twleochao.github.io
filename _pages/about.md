I'm Leo (Yi-Ting), an MSCS student at UC San Diego (B.S. Computer Science, UC Irvine).

I'm interested in ML training and interpretability, with a focus on understanding what models learn and how to measure it realiably. At UCSD, I do interpretability research on music language models with Xin Xu at the McAuley Lab. Previously, I did research on LLMs in education at the ARCALA Lab, and interned at Acer and LSEV USA Corp.
